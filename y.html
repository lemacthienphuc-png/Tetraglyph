In Chloe, a great city, the people who move through the streets are all strangers.

At each encounter, they imagine a thousand things about one another; meetings which could take place between them, conversations, surprises, caresses, bites.

But no one greets anyone; eyes lock for a second, then dart away, seeking other eyes,

never stopping...

A cooling rain is falling; a woman sways her hips

adjusting a shawl of embroidered silk; a young man displays his tattooed chest...
